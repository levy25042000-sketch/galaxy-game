VENUS — THE SCARLET DOME

Captain, Venus is a volcanic obstacle course beneath a dense, crushing atmosphere. The route crosses isolated basalt platforms suspended above glowing magma channels. The ground is unstable, the heat distorts visibility, and horned creatures guard the upper ridge.

Terrain assessment
• Molten fissures: there is no safe continuous floor. Time each jump between floating rock slabs; falling into the lava resets your position and costs a heart.
• Unstable platforms: some slabs sit at different heights. Build momentum, jump early, and avoid firing while you need precise landings.
• Ember packs: EMP is useful when several fire-creatures crowd a narrow landing. Shield is safer when a jump forces you through an enemy. Scan helps reveal armour before engaging.

Venus field note Surface temperatures average about 467°C, and surface pressure is roughly 93 times Earth’s sea-level pressure. The lava channels in this game are stylised volcanic hazards, not a claim that the entire surface is covered by active flowing lava.

Bunny’s recommendation Keep all three skills available. Prioritise survival over clearing every enemy. The three skill buttons stay together in one row.

Controls A/D or ←/→ move · Space jump · X/K fire · 1 Shield · 2 EMP · 3 Scan.
